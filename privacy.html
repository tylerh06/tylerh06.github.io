Privacy policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

ATRIOS is a personal tool that Tyler Huynh runs on his own computer for his own use. This page explains what it does with Google account data.

What ATRIOS accesses

With your permission, ATRIOS uses the Google Calendar API to read your calendars and events, and to create or change events you approve inside the app.

How the data is used

Calendar data is used only to display your schedule and plan study time within ATRIOS. It is not used for advertising, sold, or shared with anyone.

Where the data is stored

Access tokens and synced calendar data are stored locally on the computer running ATRIOS. Nothing is sent to a server operated by ATRIOS, because there isn't one.

Removing access

You can revoke ATRIOS's access at any time from your Google Account under Security, then Third-party apps and services. Deleting the ATRIOS data folder removes everything it stored locally.

Google API Services User Data Policy

ATRIOS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
